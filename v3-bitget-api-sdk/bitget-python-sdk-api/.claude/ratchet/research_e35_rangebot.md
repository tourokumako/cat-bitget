## §1 レジサポ検出・箱の定義（角度1）
- **C1 [confirmed 2-1] S/R は統計的に実在**（arXiv 2101.07410）: 単純な発見アルゴで見つけた intraday S/R が **統計的有意に価格反転**を起こす。AR(1) では説明できない＝S/R は実在の特徴（一時的予測可能性に寄与）。→ レンジ逆張りの前提（箱の端で反発）は myth でなく実在。但し票 2-1＝中程度。
- **C2 [confirmed 3-0] 高度な S/R 検出は単純版にほぼ勝てない**（arXiv 2507.01971）: attention系 DeepSupp 0.554 ≒ HMM 0.550 ≒ **単純 Local Minima 0.507**。→ **箱検出を作り込みすぎるな**＝swing/局所安値（＋出来高 POC）で十分。E34 で 5m swing pivot を使ったのは方向として正しい。
- **C3 [confirmed 3-0] その S/R 研究は検出品質のみ＝バックテストでない**（同上）: Sharpe/net/DD/コスト分析なし・BTC/5m でもない。→ **検出品質 ≠ net 黒字**。S/R が「実在」でも「コスト後に儲かる」証拠は別。
- **R1 [refuted 1-2] タッチ回数＝信頼性、は未検証**（arXiv 2101.07410）: 「過去バウンス回数が多い帯ほど次も反発」は反証側多数。→ **touch-count を強さ指標に使わない**（E34 レジサポ特徴の nearest_level_touch_count は外す根拠）。retail の「3回タッチ＝強い」も「テストされるほど弱くなる（いずれブレイク）」と矛盾。
- **R2/R3 [refuted 0-3] 「S/R 特徴で ML 利益 +65%」は反証**（MDPI 2227-7390/10/20/3888）: 8通貨ペアで S/R 特徴追加が利益 +65%・有意、という主張は **3票全部が反証**＝信じない。→ E34 iter3 で「レジサポを ML 特徴に足したら過剰適合（両窓不一致）」と実データで出たのと整合＝レジサポ特徴の万能視は危険。

## §2 レンジ vs トレンドのゲート（角度2・E35 の生命線）
- **C4 [confirmed 3-0] Jump Model (JM) > HMM**（arXiv 2402.05272）: JM は**遷移ごとに jump penalty** を課して regime の持続性を上げる＝**flicker/lag を直接抑える**。→ E35 のレンジ有効ゲートは plain HMM でなく **JM 系（jump penalty で粘らせる）**が正解形。
- **C5 [confirmed 3-0] JM の penalty は手調整でなく CV で選ぶ・return由来特徴のみ（leak無）**（同上）: jump penalty を **time-series CV で戦略性能直接最適化**・return から作る risk/return 特徴のみ。→ 固定値手調整禁止（forbid_fixed_value_tuning）と完全に整合＝penalty は CV で。
- **C6 [confirmed 2-1] JM 戦略は HMM・buy&hold を OOS（コスト・遅延込み）で上回る**（同上・US/独/日 株 1990-2023）: vol・最大DD・Sharpe で一貫優位。→ regime ゲートはコスト後でも効く実証（但し株・5m crypto でない／票 2-1）。
- **C7 [confirmed 3-0] Hurst は長系列が要る**（PMC3204962）: bias/SD<0.05 は **N≥512** のみ・**N<256 は信用不可**。
- **C8 [confirmed 1-1] Hurst 短窓は誤差±0.3**（同上）: N=128 で ±2SD が ±0.3＝**短窓 H≈0.5 では regime 判定不可**。
- **R4 [refuted 1-2] 「H 差0.1 を分離するに 32,768点要る」**（同上）: 数値は反証側だが、方向（短窓 Hurst は粗くて不可）は C7/C8 と一致。
- → **E35 結論: Hurst を 5m 短窓ゲートに使わない**（使うなら≥1.8日窓＝lag 増）。ゲートは Choppiness/ADX/箱内包＋**JM**（持続性）で組む。

## §3 maker 約定・S/R 反発の入口（角度3・★最重要の構造発見）
- **C9 [confirmed 3-0] maker 指値の逆選択は実在**（arXiv 2502.18625・BTC perp）: **fill しやすい指値ほど fill 後リターンがマイナス**＝「次5秒がマイナスの注文は高確率で約定、プラスの注文は約定しにくい」。→ **受け身の指値は負け玉（落ち続ける knife）を優先的に掴む**。E34 N11（SL 主因＝方向ミス）の厳密な裏付け。
- **R5 [unverified 1-0] 決定論的逆選択**（同上）: top-of-queue は「次が逆行なら確率1で約定」＝**ブレイク/オーバーシュート直前に集中して約定**。検証未完だが C9 と同論文・同方向。
- **R6 [unverified 0-0] 素朴な両建て MM は赤字**（同上）: BTC perp 3日で **約−60%・年率 Sharpe −109**。**儲かる maker は order-book imbalance に逆らう contrarian**。検証未完（上限）だが striking＝**「両サイドに受け身指値を置くだけ」は破綻**＝E35 の両方向オシレーションは「受け身の両建て」でなく**反転確認つき contrarian** で組む必要。
- → **E35 結論: 受け身の指値を箱の端に置くだけ＝逆選択で死ぬ**。反転確認（候補足が反転した後にエントリー＝passively rest しない）＋レンジ有効ゲートで「契機」を選ぶ。Gemini の「指値をライン手前に」より、**「反転を確認してから入る」**が厳密ソースの示す対策。

## §4 過剰適合・検証の罠（角度4・⚠️ R7-R16 は de Prado 定説＝unverified だが本物）
> これらは vote 0-0＝**上限で検証failed＝反証でない**。出典は de Prado / Bailey の一次論文＝定説。本プロジェクトの合格基準 DSR>0.95 / PBO<0.5 の出典そのもの。
- **R7-R10 [unverified] DSR / 多重検定**（SSRN 2460551）: 観測 Sharpe は「何回試したか」を無視＝過大。**N 回試行で真 Sharpe=0 でも E[max Sharpe] は N とともに増える**（公式: (1−γ)Φ⁻¹(1−1/N)+γΦ⁻¹(1−1/(Ne))）。hold-out 単独は選択バイアスを補正しない。
- **R11-R16 [unverified] PBO / CSCV / 過剰適合**（SSRN 2326253 / escholarship 4w1110bb）: **IS Sharpe と OOS Sharpe は負の関係**（最適化するほど OOS 悪化）。実例 IS 100%正(1〜3) なのに **OOS 78%が負**。**純粋ランダムウォークにグリッド探索（8,800通り）で年率 Sharpe 1.27・PSR<1% を捏造でき、CSCV では PBO=55%・OOS 53%が負**。
- → **E35 結論**: 5m レンジ bot をパラメータ・グリッドで小サンプル最適化すると、**ノイズから「有意な」Sharpe を作れてしまう**。だから本プロジェクトの掟（①固定値スイープ禁止＝木に学習・②学習/採点/封印の分離・③連続モード×年別・④bootstrap 0またぎ・⑤score.py 1回）が正しい。E34 の N5 bootstrap 0またぎ警告はこの理論の実装。

## §5 ★E35 設計への落とし込み（actionable・各決定にソース紐付け）
1. **箱検出は単純に**（C2）: swing/局所安値＋出来高 POC で十分。多TF合議は「重なり」程度に留め、凝った検出器・ML 箱検出は作らない。
2. **touch-count を強さ特徴にしない**（R1・E34 iter3 整合）: 強さは「出来高 POC の濃さ」「多TF で同価格が重なるか」で見る（touch 回数でない）。
3. **regime ゲートは JM（jump penalty）＋ Choppiness/ADX/箱内包**（C4/C5/C6）。**Hurst は短窓で使わない**（C7/C8/R4）。penalty は CV で選ぶ＝固定値手調整しない。
4. **受け身の指値を置かない＝逆選択で knife を掴む**（C9/R5/R6）。入口は **反転確認後**（反転足/stoch 反転/直近安値切上げ）にアクティブに置く。「両サイドに受け身指値」＝素朴 MM は赤字（R6）。
5. **両方向オシレーションは contrarian＝レンジ有効時のみ**（R6/C9）。トレンドでは flat（ゲート）。
6. **過剰適合を構造で防ぐ**（R7-R16）: 固定値スイープ禁止（木/CV）・学習/採点/封印分離・連続モード×年別・bootstrap・score.py 1回。**小サンプル多パラメータ最適化はノイズから Sharpe を作る**＝E35 のパラメータは最小限・a priori。
7. **検出品質 ≠ 黒字**（C3）: 「箱がうまく引けた」で満足せず、必ず連続モード score.py の net＋コスト後で判定。

### A. レジサポ検出・箱（24件）
1. [確](票2-1) Support/resistance levels are statistically real price barriers that reverse price trends, not artifacts explainable by AR(1) processes — i.e., a heuristic discovery algorithm finds intraday SR levels that reverse trends with statistical significance.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2101.07410
2. [確](票3-0) On a weighted combination of six SR-quality metrics, the attention-based DeepSupp (0.554 +/- 0.039) only marginally beats an HMM baseline (0.550 +/- 0.044) and a simple Local Minima detector (0.507 +/- 0.048) — i.e., the sophisticated method's edge over basic baselines is small.  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2507.01971v1
3. [反](票1-2) A level's prior touch/bounce count predicts future reliability: levels that have produced more prior price bounces are more likely to produce another bounce when price returns. This validates touch-count as a strength/reliability metric.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2101.07410
4. [反](票0-3) Adding engineered support/resistance level features to an otherwise-identical machine-learning trading model increased aggregate profitability by 65% across eight currency pairs, demonstrating that S/R levels carry real predictive edge as model inputs (not just retail myth).  — 出典: www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/10
5. [反](票0-3) The profitability difference attributable to S/R features was statistically significant, not noise — the profitability distributions of the with-S/R vs without-S/R models differed significantly, supporting that the S/R contribution is a genuine effect.  — 出典: www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/10
6. [未](票未到達) SR levels decay with age: the probability of a price bounce at a discovered level decreases over time, so older/stale levels are less reliable than recent ones.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2101.07410
7. [未](票未到達) SR-level reliability is demonstrated on intraday price series across multiple non-crypto asset classes (EUR/USD forex, Lloyds equity, Brent crude commodity), so the touch-count and decay findings are general intraday phenomena not yet validated specifically on BTC or the 5-minute timeframe.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2101.07410
8. [未](票未到達) The repo explicitly frames ADX as the regime gate for a mean-reversion strategy: the strategy is intended for sideways/choppy markets and ADX is used specifically to detect and avoid trending markets, while Bollinger Bands locate reversal entries. This supports ADX-as-range-filter as a documented practitioner pattern.  — 出典: github.com/jasgin/backtrader-backtests
9. [未](票未到達) The Stochastic + Donchian S/R strategy places a dual stop-loss: one at a fixed max-loss distance (0.002, i.e. 20 pips) AND one at the prior Donchian support/resistance level (max high / min low over the lookback period), as OCO orders. Exits use stochastic %D thresholds (close long when %D >= 70, close short when %D <= 30; enter short on %D crossing back below 80, long on crossing back above 20). This is a concrete box-break-invalidation stop placement using S/R levels.  — 出典: github.com/jasgin/backtrader-backtests
10. [未](票1-0) Maker limit orders at the top-of-book are subject to deterministic adverse selection: if the next price move is against a resting top-of-queue order, it fills with probability 1, meaning passive limit fills are concentrated precisely when the level is about to be broken/overshot.  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2502.18625v2
11. [未](票未到達) Fill probability is predictable from order-book state via an OLS model (z = 0.5649 - 0.0159*Q_near + 0.1013*Q_opp - 0.3166*imb), where a larger opposite-side queue and order-book imbalance raise fill probability while a larger near-side queue lowers it, giving concrete leak-free features for timing maker limit entries near a level.  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2502.18625v2
12. [未](票未到達) The Hurst exponent provides a single scalar regime classifier: H<0.5 indicates mean reversion (anti-persistence), H=0.5 indicates a random walk / geometric Brownian motion, and H>0.5 indicates trending (persistence); values near 0 are strongly mean-reverting and near 1 strongly trending. This directly supports gating a mean-reversion bot to trade only when H is well below 0.5.  — 出典: macrosynergy.com/research/detecting-trends-and-mean-reversion-with-the-hurst-exponent
13. [未](票未到達) Mean-reversion-style entries near local minima exist in BTC but are extremely risky: returns are stronger when price is over the local minima but the strategies suffer drawdowns over 80%, indicating that naive 'buy the dip / bounce off support' logic is hazardous even before costs.  — 出典: quantpedia.com/trend-following-and-mean-reversion-in-bitcoin/
14. [未](票未到達) Across tested daily lookback windows of 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 days, the SHORTEST lookbacks (10-day) performed best for the trend-following component — relevant to the general design question of how reactive a level/regime window should be.  — 出典: quantpedia.com/trend-following-and-mean-reversion-in-bitcoin/
15. [未](票未到達) DeepSupp detects dynamic support levels using a four-stage pipeline: volume-weighted features, Spearman rank correlation matrices over sliding windows (n=32), a multi-head attention autoencoder (4 heads, 32-dim embedding compressed to 16), then DBSCAN clustering (epsilon=0.1, min_samples=10% of dataset).  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2507.01971v1
16. [未](票未到達) On raw support-accuracy, the simple Local Minima detector (0.603) outperforms the deep-learning DeepSupp method (0.483); DeepSupp's overall lead comes from price-proximity and support-duration rather than detection accuracy.  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2507.01971v1
17. [未](票未到達) The paper makes no explicit treatment of touch-count validation, multi-timeframe confluence, or data-leakage/causal safeguards when defining SR levels.  — 出典: arxiv.org/html/2507.01971v1
18. [未](票未到達) Breakout failure rates are very high: 60-80% across traditional markets and an estimated 60-70% in crypto due to 24/7 trading, lower liquidity, and manipulation risk. This is central evidence that a mean-reversion bot should treat most level-breaks as fadeable rather than trade them as continuation.  — 出典: chartscout.io/how-to-spot-fake-breakouts-crypto
19. [未](票未到達) Entry should wait for a full candle close beyond the level, and a long upper wick (more than twice the candle body, ratio >2:1) signals rejection, with >3:1 almost always preceding a reversal. This is a concrete candle-close/wick confirmation rule applicable to S/R-bounce entries.  — 出典: chartscout.io/how-to-spot-fake-breakouts-crypto
20. [未](票未到達) The Bitcoin market is empirically weak-form inefficient at the intraday level, consistent with the adaptive market hypothesis — meaning predictable, exploitable short-horizon patterns can exist but are conditional on time period and venue.  — 出典: www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles
21. [未](票未到達) No single parameterization of the momentum strategy generated the greatest cumulative return across all sample periods, which is direct academic evidence of parameter instability/overfitting risk in HF crypto strategies — a documented failure mode for any threshold-based bot (range gate cutoffs, level-strength thresholds, etc.).  — 出典: www.researchgate.net/publication/337998137_High_frequency_momentum_trading_with_cryptocurrencies
22. [未](票未到達) Signal-based momentum strategies (using exponential moving averages) outperformed simple returns-based momentum strategies, suggesting that confirmation/filtering via smoothed signals adds value over raw return signals in HF crypto — analogous to using close/EMA-confirmed entries rather than raw price-touch triggers.  — 出典: www.researchgate.net/publication/337998137_High_frequency_momentum_trading_with_cryptocurrencies
23. [未](票未到達) The paper introduces an automated, algorithmic methodology for identifying meaningful support/resistance price levels (rather than manual chart reading), framing S/R detection as an engineerable, reproducible feature pipeline tied to psychological price levels.  — 出典: www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/10
24. [未](票未到達) The motivation is that algorithmic trading models had converged on near-identical input feature sets, so the marginal edge now comes from novel features (like S/R) rather than from more complex model architectures — relevant to prioritizing feature engineering over model complexity.  — 出典: www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/10

### B. レンジ/トレンド判定（25件）
25. [確](票3-0) The statistical jump model (JM) distinguishes itself from traditional Markov-switching / HMM regime models by enhancing regime persistence via a jump penalty applied at each state transition, which directly addresses the regime-flickering / lag problem that plagues mean-reversion bots' range-vs-trend gating.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2402.05272
26. [確](票3-0) The jump penalty hyperparameter that controls regime stickiness is not hand-tuned but selected via time-series cross-validation that directly optimizes downstream strategy performance, using only return-derived risk/return features (no leak-prone external inputs).  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2402.05272
27. [確](票2-1) In realistic out-of-sample testing on US, German, and Japanese equity indices over 1990-2023 that explicitly includes transaction costs and trading delays, the JM-guided regime strategy consistently outperformed both an HMM-guided strategy and buy-and-hold on volatility, maximum drawdown, and Sharpe ratio.  — 出典: arxiv.org/abs/2402.05272
28. [確](票3-0) Reliable Hurst exponent estimation requires fairly long series: all three SWV methods achieve bias and standard deviation below 0.05 only for N >= 2^9 (512) points, and estimates for series shorter than N < 2^8 (256) are unreliable.  — 出典: www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles
29. [確](票1-1) Hurst estimates from short windows carry very large error bars: for N = 2^7 (128) points the +/-2 SD confidence interval spans +/-0.3 or more around the true H, so a single short-window estimate near 0.5 cannot confidently classify a regime.  — 出典: www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles
30. [反](票1-2) Distinguishing two regimes whose true Hurst values differ by 0.1 at 95% confidence requires more than 2^15 (about 32,768) data points, implying coarse short-window Hurst estimates cannot reliably separate persistent (trending) from anti-persistent (mean-reverting) regimes.  — 出典: www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles
31. [未](票未到達) The Bollinger Bands + ADX mean-reversion strategy uses an ADX trend filter to only enter when ADX is below a max threshold (default 40), with BB period 20 and 2 standard deviations; entry is a re-cross of the band back toward the mean, take-profit at the BB middle (mean) and stop-loss at the BB band value on entry. This is a concrete, codified template for the research question's range-gating + bounce-entry + mean-target design.  — 出典: github.com/jasgin/backtrader-backtests
32. [未](票未到達) Manufacturing a high in-sample Sharpe is trivially easy with even a small number of parameters and a few hundred optimization iterations, and out-of-sample performance is then 'utterly disappointing' - a concrete warning against over-tuning the small number of knobs (box width, ADX/Hurst thresholds, limit offset, TP) on a range bot.  — 出典: escholarship.org/uc/item
33. [未](票未到達) A strategy profitable through a bull market can bleed continuously once the regime shifts to a bear or low-volatility range, illustrating regime-shift failure mode that backtests cannot predict.  — 出典: bitsgap.com/blog/crypto-bot-backtesting-in-2026-what-it-shows-and-what-it-cannot-predict
34. [未](票未到達) Applying a 2-state Gaussian HMM regime filter to a trend-following strategy improved risk metrics modestly but its primary benefit was drawdown reduction, not return enhancement: Sharpe rose 0.37 to 0.48 and CAGR rose 6.41% to 6.88%, while maximum daily drawdown fell from ~56% to ~24%.  — 出典: www.quantstart.com/articles/market-regime-detection-using-hidden-markov-models-in-qstrader
35. [未](票未到達) The HMM regime filter works mainly by keeping the strategy flat during adverse (high-volatility/crisis) regimes — it caused the strategy to not trade at all from early 2008 to mid 2009 and reduced total trades from 41 to 31.  — 出典: www.quantstart.com/articles/market-regime-detection-using-hidden-markov-models-in-qstrader
36.
